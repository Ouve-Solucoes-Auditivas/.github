# OUVE Soluções Auditivas

**Hearing Healthcare · Proprietary Technology · Artificial Intelligence**

OUVE Soluções Auditivas is a Brazilian hearing healthcare company founded in October 2026, based in Uberlândia, Minas Gerais.

We combine hearing healthcare services with proprietary technology developed to support our internal operations.

## Our Technology

We are building an integrated internal technology ecosystem focused on improving operational efficiency, patient communication, and the hearing care journey.

Our development initiatives include:

- **Proprietary CRM:** Internal relationship management, lead tracking, and operational workflows.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Conversational AI and intelligent assistance for communication and administrative processes.
- **Workflow Automation:** Integration of internal systems, services, and operational processes.
- **Digital Hearing Care:** Development of digital workflows supporting preliminary screening and patient journeys.
- **Decision Intelligence:** Research and experimentation with intelligent decision models.

## Our Engineering Approach

Our technology initiatives use modern software engineering practices, API integrations, modular services, and secure data management.

We prioritize reliability, maintainability, access control, and the responsible handling of healthcare-related information.

## Proprietary Software

All technology is owned by OUVE Soluções Auditivas and developed exclusively for our internal operations.

Our core applications and infrastructure repositories are private. This public profile provides an overview of our technology initiatives without exposing proprietary source code, credentials, or sensitive information.
